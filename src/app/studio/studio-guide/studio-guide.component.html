workspace_premium Five rules that beat plugins

  1. Finish one loop before starting another. A finished rough idea teaches more than ten perfect openings.
  2. Mix quiet. Balance at conversation volume; loudness hides bad decisions.
  3. Cut low end from everything that is not kick or bass. Mud is the most common amateur signature.
  4. Check on more than one speaker. Phone, headphones, car. If it works in all three, it works.
  5. Sleep before mastering. Fresh ears catch what excitement cannot.
